Okanzo is a private, offline-first Android app for tracking your net worth - your assets minus your liabilities - organised into portfolios.

Your financial data is stored encrypted on your device and never leaves it, except when you deliberately export an encrypted backup.

Features:

* Track stocks, funds, crypto, valuables, cash, bank deposits, property, vehicles, bonds, and more.
* Value metals, gems and jewellery by weight with an editable per-gram rate, or enter a total appraisal. Supported metals offer indicative purity-adjusted futures quotes, not retail prices.
* Fetch current prices for market-linked assets by ticker symbol.
* Search stocks, funds and crypto by company/coin name or ticker, with listing and trading-pair suggestions from Yahoo Finance.
* Use fetched company/coin names or optional custom names, and fetch missing market prices automatically on Save.
* Select stock exchanges across the US, China, Europe, Japan, Hong Kong, and India (BSE and NSE), and save a preferred-exchange shortlist.
* Look up full Yahoo Finance tickers for other markets and cryptocurrency pairs.
* Track loans, mortgages, credit cards, pending payments, and taxes.
* See total assets, liabilities, and net worth in the home overview, with separate Assets and Liabilities tabs.
* Optionally shorten the net-worth headline with currency-specific units, including lakh/crore for INR.
* Create uniquely named portfolios and switch between them with clear Viewing and Default badges.
* Open your default portfolio on launch and switch immediately when the default changes.
* Search holdings with Quick-Find, collapse categories and see totals, counts and percentage shares.
* Reorder categories and holdings, or sort holdings by name or value without losing your saved manual order.
* Inspect valuation provenance, converted values and portfolio percentage shares in holding details.
* Keep precise amount inputs; Save stays disabled when a value would lose digits.
* Convert holdings using cached exchange rates.
* Encrypt the local database with SQLCipher and protect its key with Android Keystore.
* Optionally lock every screen and dialog with fingerprint or device credentials, and require authentication for lock-setting changes and protected exports. Screenshots and recent-apps previews are blocked by default (configurable in Settings).
* Hide balances and holding quantities across Home, Statistics and Portfolios with synchronized eye controls.
* Export and import password-protected encrypted backups.
* Use the warm Mineral Ledger light theme or deep-green Midnight dark theme, or follow your device. Motion respects reduced-animation settings.
* Find preferences in grouped settings and enter holdings with outlined fields, searchable pickers, guided focus, live values and a pinned Save action.
* Review centered save confirmations, undo an addition or update, or add another holding without losing draft inputs.
* See release highlights after updating, or reopen them from Settings. App information has its own About dialog.
* Choose from 21 app languages in Settings, or follow your device language.
* Open the themed app sooner while encrypted holdings load in the background.

Okanzo is offline-first. Network access is only used to look up public market prices, company/coin/ticker suggestions and exchange rates. Only a ticker symbol, text typed into the stock/fund/crypto search field, or base currency code is sent. There are no accounts, analytics, or trackers, and portfolio values, asset name fields, quantities and holdings are never transmitted.

Note on network use: currency exchange rates are refreshed automatically in the background - on first launch and then at most once every 12 hours - so your net worth is always shown correctly in your chosen base currency. Prices are fetched when you refresh, fetch a price, select a market listing or metal purity, or save a market-linked asset without a price. Custom rates and total appraisals are not refreshed automatically. Search sends text to Yahoo Finance after you type at least two characters and pause briefly. Manual ticker entry remains available when search is unavailable.
